ACCUM-A is a long-only accumulation bot for the make.fun BTC Race 24h competition (btc-race-24h-20261118-1444, cbBTC vs USDC, 1-minute bars, $1,000 paper). It enters on a 75 bps flush below the 90-minute SMA with 15-minute RSI(2) under 20, or on at least two of four 15-minute mean-reversion legs while the market is flat (15-minute ATR under 20 bps of price), buying 25% of capital ($250) per tranche and adding another 25% each time price drops 1% from the last fill (max 4 tranches), and exits on a 3% take-profit, a stop, a trailing stop that arms at +2%, or a 4-hour time stop counted from the first buy, all computed from the qty-weighted average entry. On this competition's frozen 24-hour dev window (a falling market, -2.80%) it made 0 trades for a net PnL of $0.00, so it does not pass the dev gate; the same strategy made +$4.44 on the prior competition's dev window (1 trade, trailing-stop exit).
